Projects

Project 01

Voting System

I built a simple web-based voting system for Libertad National High School that lets students log in with their Student ID and cast one vote per election. It replaces manual paper ballots with a faster, more organized process, and gives admins a dashboard to manage candidates, parties, voters, and vote counts. It prevents double voting by tracking each voter's status.

Role: Developer (backend and frontend)

Tools: Python, Django, HTML, CSS, JavaScript
